# Build Mode

Use when starting fresh, when existing content is weak or generic, or when the user wants to evolve the voice strategically. Treat answers as intended voice, not evidence of established practice.

## Strategic Questions to Ask

**Identity**
1. What are 3–5 words that describe your personality?
2. What do you stand for? What is your core belief about your industry or topic?
3. What is your background? What shaped how you see things?
4. What makes you genuinely different from others in your space?

**Audience**
5. Who are you talking to? Be specific.
6. What tone resonates with them? What do they respond to?
7. What would make them trust you? What would turn them off?

**Positioning**
8. Are you the expert, peer, rebel, guide, or insider?
9. Where do you sit on accessible ↔ exclusive?
10. Where do you sit on approachable ↔ authoritative?

**Aspiration**
11. Name 2–3 brands or people whose voice you admire. What specifically do you like about how they communicate?
12. What do you explicitly not want to sound like?

**Practical**
13. Any words or phrases that are signature to you?
14. Any words or phrases you hate or want to avoid?
15. How do you feel about humor, profanity, and hot takes?

## Build Process

From the answers, construct the voice profile:
1. Synthesize personality into core traits that should come through.
2. Define positions on the tone spectrum.
3. Set vocabulary rules: what to use and avoid.
4. Establish sentence and paragraph rhythm.
5. Create sample phrases that embody the voice.
6. Define off-brand boundaries.
7. Map platform adaptations, using the guide referenced by the main skill.

Proceed to the main skill's Voice Test Loop.

**Done:** The answers support every profile dimension or the unresolved choices are presented for user confirmation before drafting.

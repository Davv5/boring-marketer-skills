# Worked voice-profile examples

Read when a concrete example will help distinguish evidence from interpretation. These are illustrative, not defaults to copy into a user's profile.

## Extract example: founder

Input: a SaaS founder's website, tweets, and newsletters.

- **Observed:** Casual language, specific numbers, short paragraphs, candid failures, and direct reader address.
- **Synthesis:** A peer-builder voice: self-deprecating confidence, action-oriented, transparent, and accessible.
- **Profile consequence:** Prefer grounded numbers and real lessons; avoid corporate abstractions and unsupported certainty.

## Build example: coach

Input: a new coaching business; the user wants directness and warmth, rejects hustle culture, and describes an experienced-peer stance.

- **Intent:** Calm, supportive directness; accessible language; sustainable success.
- **Profile consequence:** Permission-giving phrasing and actionable advice; avoid hype, guarantees, and grind language.

## Scrape example: public creator

Input: a public website, accessible social posts, and newsletter samples.

- **Observed:** Record source coverage and repeated language patterns; separate those observations from preferences elicited in follow-up questions.
- **Profile consequence:** A profile can use public-source patterns while marking the user's requested evolution as intent rather than pretending it already appears in the source.

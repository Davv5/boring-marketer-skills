# Platform adaptation guide

Read while completing the Platform Adaptations table. These are starting considerations, not defaults to copy: derive each brand's shift from its evidence and confirm unsupported choices with the user.

| Platform | Consider | Typical structure and length |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Warmer, personal relationship; natural contractions and sign-off | Short paragraphs, one clear CTA; usually 150–300 words, longer for story-driven sequences |
| LinkedIn | Expertise and substance without stiffness | Single idea, early hook, readable line breaks; usually 100–200 words |
| Twitter/X | Concision and a stronger point of view where authentic | One idea per post; threads for stories; each post under 280 characters |
| Blog/SEO | Same personality with greater depth and nuance | Headings, examples, longer explanations; usually 1,500–2,500 words |
| Landing page | Benefits and proof made clear while preserving voice | Short sections and clear CTAs; length varies by section |

# Newsletter platform guidance

Read when the user names an ESP or asks for platform advice. Verify current pricing and features before presenting them; product details change. Treat the following as positioning, not a guarantee.

- **Beehiiv:** growth and media-business tools, including referrals and advertising options; assess branding, integrations, and the user's need for those tools.
- **Substack:** simple publishing and built-in discovery/social features; account for its paid-subscription revenue share and limited customization.
- **Kit (formerly ConvertKit):** creator commerce, tagging, and automation; assess design needs and cost as the list grows.
- **Ghost:** publication, membership, and ownership control; weigh hosting and technical overhead against platform independence.

For formatting, use the platform's current editor conventions. Prefer simple, portable formatting unless the user requests platform-specific elements. Confirm that embeds, buttons, polls, personalization, and paid-content features are supported before relying on them.

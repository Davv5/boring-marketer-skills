# News briefing

A scannable, timely selection of important developments.

## Template

```text
Subject: [date]: [hook about the lead story]

Today: [one-line preview]

## [Lead story]
[What happened, linked source, why it matters]

## [Next story]
[What happened, linked source, key implication]

## Quick hits
- [Brief item and source]

[One useful engagement question and sign-off]
```

## Example

A briefing for independent retailers leads with a linked policy change, explains its likely operational effect without overstating it, then gives two brief sourced developments and one practical question for readers.

# Cart-abandonment Format

Read during sequence planning for an ecommerce cart left without purchase. Plan three recovery emails; take the timing from `references/send-timing.md` rather than treating this as a generic welcome series.

## Email purposes and file names

1. `01-cart-reminder.md` — remind the shopper what they left and provide a working return-to-cart link.
2. `02-cart-objection.md` — address a supported purchase objection and make checkout easy.
3. `03-cart-follow-up.md` — give a final useful follow-up with one clear next action.

Confirm the cart, product, checkout URL, and any incentive or expiry with the user or connected platform. Use urgency only when a real deadline or availability constraint exists. When configuring automation, confirm how completed purchases stop further recovery emails before activation.

Completion: three emails have distinct purposes and the specified timing, accurate product/offer facts, and one recovery CTA each.
